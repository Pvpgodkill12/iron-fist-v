HOW TO PLAY & COMMAND LIST

💻 Player 1 Keyboard & Mouse

  • Move / Guard: W A S D or Arrow Keys
  • 3D Sidestep: Quick tap W (In) or S (Out)
  • 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 Attacks: Keys 1 2 3 4, or Z X C V, or U I J K
  • Easy Signature Special: Q
  • Easy Juggle Launcher: E
  • Rage Art / Power Crush: Space or R or O
  • Throw / Throw Break: F or Y
  • Mouse: Left-Click Arena = Punch, Right-Click = Kick

👥 Player 2 Keyboard & Gamepads

  • P2 Move: Arrow Keys
  • P2 1 / 2 / 3 / 4: Numpad 4 / 8 / 1 / 2 or 7 / 8 / 9 / 0
  • P2 1+2 / 3+4: Numpad 6 / 3 or - / =
  • PS5 / Xbox Pad: □/X = 1, △/Y = 2, ✕/A = 3, ○/B = 4
  • Pad Triggers: R1 = 1+2 Rage, R2 = 3+4, L1 = 1+3 Throw

📱 Mobile Touch & Combat Tips

  • Left Thumb Stick: Drag right to advance, hold left to Block, tap up/down to 3D Sidestep!
  • Right Buttons: Tap 1 2 3 4 for strings, 🚀 LAUNCH then mash 1, 2, 4 for aerial juggles, and ⚡ SPECIAL for signature finishers!
  • Rage Art: Below 25% HP your lifebar blazes crimson — tap 1+2 RAGE (or Space) for an armored cinematic super!
